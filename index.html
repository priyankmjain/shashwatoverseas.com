About Shashwat Overseas

Shashwat Overseas LLP is a trusted name in the Indian textile industry, rooted in Surat, Gujarat — the nation's undisputed textile capital. With over 30 years of industry experience, we have built deep expertise in sourcing and manufacturing premium ladies wear — from timeless ethnic classics to contemporary western styles.

Our operations are structured as an export-oriented setup, enabling us to meet the quality standards and delivery requirements of both domestic and international clients with consistent excellence.

At the heart of everything we do is a singular commitment: Customer Satisfaction above all. From the first consultation to final delivery, every interaction is guided by transparency, reliability, and respect for your business goals.

We believe that Perfection in Every Creation is not just our tagline — it is the promise we honour with every thread, every stitch, and every shipment.
